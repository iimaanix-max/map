6. Advertising, Analytics & Ad Tracker Deletion Guide

The app displays advertisements via Google AdMob to fund ongoing server and development costs. All advertisements across Digital Tasbih are strictly restricted to G-Rating (General Audiences), ensuring clean, family-friendly, and wholesome content suitable for an Islamic application.

📱 How to Delete Your Google Advertising ID (Ad Tracker) in 1 Tap

Third-party advertising identifiers are managed directly by Google Play Services on your device. Neither Imaanix nor any other app developer can delete Google's internal logs, but you can permanently delete or reset your advertising identifier directly from your phone:

  1. Open your phone's Android Settings.
  2. Scroll down and tap Google (or Google Services).
  3. Tap on Ads (or Privacy • Ads).
  4. Tap Delete Advertising ID (or Reset Advertising ID) and confirm.

Result: Google immediately destroys your device's persistent advertising ID. Ad networks can no longer build cross-app advertising profiles for your phone.

9. Security Safeguards & Backup Exclusions

All communication between Digital Tasbih and cloud servers is enforced over HTTPS / TLS 1.3 encryption; unencrypted cleartext traffic is strictly blocked by our network security configuration. Cloud databases utilize Google Cloud's AES-256 encryption at rest.

🛡️ Android Cloud Backup Protection

Standard Android Auto-Backup often uploads app data to personal Google Drive accounts. To protect your privacy, Digital Tasbih's backup_rules.xml and data_extraction_rules.xml explicitly exclude sensitive contact files (dt_campaign_prefs.xml) and GPS location caches (dt_prayer_prefs.xml) from cloud backup. Your phone number, name, and GPS coordinates are never uploaded to unencrypted cloud drive backups.
