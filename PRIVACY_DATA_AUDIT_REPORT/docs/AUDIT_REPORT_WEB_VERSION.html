1. Executive Summary & Architecture Topology

Digital Tasbih by Imaanix implements a distinct dual-sphere privacy architecture:

  • 100% Offline Local Sphere: The primary core Islamic utility—including the realistic Bézier beads tasbih (TasbihScreen.kt), digital counters, lifetime badge progression, daily statistics, offline Quran text reading, and daily Athkar—operates entirely inside Android's private app sandbox. No account is required, and zero user counts or prayer markers are transmitted over the internet.
  • Cloud & Community Sphere: Optional features—specifically Live Dhikr Rooms, the Mega Umrah Contest, and prayer times geolocation—connect to external cloud endpoints: Cloudflare Workers (auth-worker.js, campaigns-worker.js), Cloudflare D1 SQL, Google Cloud Firestore (digital-tasbih-campaigns-e43ee), and the Aladhan Prayer API.
CRITICAL AUDIT NOTICE (Tasbih Lock Rule): In strict compliance with project safety directives, the Tasbih module (TasbihScreen.kt, Bézier canvas math, dial progression, lifetime badges) was inspected in READ-ONLY mode and remains 100% untouched.

5. Deliverable 4 & 5: Public Visibility & Retention Reality Check

Public Visibility Breakdown:

  • Publicly Visible to Other Users: Display Name (displayName), City (city), Submitted Dhikr Count (count), Last Active Timestamp (lastActive), and a truncated HMAC mask (publicId).
  • Strictly Confidential & Never Displayed: Phone numbers (E.164), normalized email addresses, exact GPS coordinates, personal offline tasbih counts, and hardware UUIDs.

Data Retention & Deletion Reality Check:

VULNERABILITY 1: In-App "Delete My Data" is Simulated
In SettingsScreen.kt#L1151-L1155, tapping "Submit Erasure Request" inside DeleteMyDataDpdpModal only sets isSubmitted = true in memory. It makes no API call, does not delete the Firebase Auth user, and erases zero records from D1 or Firestore.
VULNERABILITY 2: No Automated Retention Cron in Cloudflare D1
In schema.sql and auth-worker.js, OTP records in table otp_records and rate limits in rate_limits remain indefinitely until overwritten by a new OTP request. No scheduled cron exists in auth-wrangler.toml.
VULNERABILITY 3: Android Auto-Backup Leaks PII to Google Drive
backup_rules.xml#L3 and data_extraction_rules.xml#L4-L9 include standard SharedPreferences. Plain-text phone numbers (dt_gc_phone), names (dt_gc_name), and GPS coordinates (pr_lat, pr_lon) are automatically uploaded unencrypted to the user's personal Google Drive.

8. Deliverable 8 & 10: Legal Questions & Top 10 Pitfalls

Essential Questions for App Owner & Legal Counsel:

  1. Legal Entity Name & Address: What is the formal registered name and address of the entity operating Imaanix?
  2. DPDP Grievance Officer: Who is the appointed Data Grievance Redressal Officer (Name, official email e.g., grievance@imaanix.in)?
  3. Public Web Deletion URL: What is the publicly accessible URL for Google Play account deletion (e.g., https://imaanix.in/delete-account)?
  4. Umrah Contest Eligibility & Tax: Is participation strictly restricted to 18+ Indian citizens, and who covers Section 194B TDS on prizes?
  5. Automated D1 Purge: When will the scheduled Cloudflare Worker cron be added to purge D1 otp_records older than 30 days?

Top 10 Things That Would Make the Policy False If Ignored:

  1. Claiming Android local storage is encrypted using Keystore when standard SharedPreferences is used.
  2. Claiming the in-app "Delete My Data" button erases server data when it is currently simulated.
  3. Claiming an automated 30-day purge for contest entries when purging is completely manual.
  4. Failing to disclose plain-text storage of phone numbers and emails in Firestore documents.
  5. Failing to disclose Brevo (Sendinblue) as an active email infrastructure provider.
  6. Ignoring Google Drive unencrypted cloud backups caused by backup_rules.xml.
  7. Failing to disclose transmission of exact GPS coordinates to the third-party Aladhan API.
  8. Claiming "zero third-party sharing" while utilizing Google AdMob and Google Play Services.
  9. Omitting the 18+ age gate for community features under the India DPDP Act 2023.
  10. Lacking a functioning, publicly accessible web URL for account and personal data deletion requests.
