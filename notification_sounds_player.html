🕌 Dedicated Prayer Alert Sounds

Special Prayer Notification Sounds

Bhai, jab namaz ka waqt hoga toh yeh short aur pyaara sound aayega (poori 3-minute Azan tabhi aayegi jab user use manually on karega). Inhe suniye:

🕌

1. Short Takbeer (Sheikh Ahmad Al-Nafees)

Short Voice (8s)

"Allahu Akbar, Allahu Akbar" — Sheikh Ahmad Al-Nafees ki rooh-parwar awaz me pehla Takbeer. Short, respectful aur beautiful.

🎙️

2. Short Takbeer (Mishary Rashid Alafasy)

Short Voice (8s)

"Allahu Akbar, Allahu Akbar" — World-famous Qari Mishary Alafasy ki opening Takbeer. Clear aur melodious.

🕋

3. Short Takbeer (Sheikh Mansour Al-Zahrani)

Short Voice (8s)

"Allahu Akbar, Allahu Akbar" — Deep, majestic Makkah style sacred Takbeer call.

🔔

4. Sacred Prayer Chime (Maqam Hijaz)

Melodic Chime (2.8s)

Traditional Islamic sacred scale (Maqam Hijaz) me banayi gayi soft bell melody. Kaan ko behad sukoon dene wali namaz ki ghanti.

✨

5. Madinah Serenity Chime

Gentle Tone (2.5s)

3-note calm harmonized tone. Ultra-minimal, modern aur silent reminder tone.

💡 Kaise chalega: User agar normal notification rakhega toh inme se choose kiya gaya short 6-8 sec sound aayega. Aur agar user "Full Adhan" select karega, tabhi poori 3 minute ki Azan chalegi!
